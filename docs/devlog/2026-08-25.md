## Broke / surprised me

- **The tripwire needed its own carry.** With plan attached by pass-0
  (current net), the mu-recompute tripwire would compare serve logp
  against logits conditioned on a *different* net's emissions — measuring
  pass-0 drift, not serve/loader drift. Fixed: the tripwire rebuilds the
  head segment's carry under the REF net before recomputing.
- `float()` on a grad tensor warns in torch 2.x — the aux raw
  accumulators needed explicit `.detach()`.
- act()-vs-forward numerics differ slightly under `no_grad` (the
  TransformerEncoder fused fast path) — the act-exposes-plan test uses
  1e-4 tolerance, not equality.
