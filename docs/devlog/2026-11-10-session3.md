## Broke / surprised me

- Proof A's `done rc=0` is the signal handler's re-entry, not a clean exit. One SIGTERM to an old supervisor over `sleep 600` records `done rc=0` six times in six. The handler signalled its own group until the recursion limit; a reap was lost on the way and the next poll read "no such child" as exit 0.
- The launch's arguments could not go on the supervisor's command line. The memory watchdog and every pattern kill classify processes by command line, and a supervisor that carried the run's command would have matched them.
- A PreToolUse hook refused a heredoc whose test comment spelled out a pattern-kill command. The comment was reworded.
