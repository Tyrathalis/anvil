## Broke / surprised me

- **The recert guard fired for a reason the pin never priced.** I set
  70% expecting to detect era drift; what it detected was selection
  regression — certified margins were chosen on old-jar noise, so
  re-measurement regresses them even under a frozen engine. The Δ split
  (+12.5 recert / −1.7 non-recert) is what rescued the read from its own
  guard. Standing rule born: a re-certification threshold on a selected
  population must price winner's-curse regression, not only drift.
- **The measurement design almost cost a subtlety:** renumbering jobs
  would have silently changed `rollSeed` and broken the cross-horizon
  pairing; reusing the revalidation ids was the fix. Worth remembering —
  the roll seed is derived from (seed, job id, roll), so job identity is
  part of the determinization contract.
- **The positive-but-subthreshold stratum reads −4.9pp (z = −2.7)** —
  small in-era board advantages converting slightly against. Recorded as
  an exploratory anomaly in the ADR, not interpreted.
- Background-Bash waiters cap at 10 min — the completion hook had to be
  a persistent Monitor instead. Worked cleanly (fired on read-landed).
