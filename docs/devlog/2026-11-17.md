## Broke / surprised me

- **The head-to-head scoreboard is not uniformly sharper than the heuristic one.** Its error is smaller at the same game count (0.55pp against 0.73pp), but its margins are not always larger: `iter-301` to `iter-405` is 3.1pp against the heuristic and 1.3pp head to head. The design doc's "more sensitive scoreboard" expectation holds for the warm-start pairs only.
- **Seat 0 is under 0.5 in all nine pairs** (0.468 to 0.495). Attributed the same day: it is not a seat effect. Seat 0 always holds the first deck of the pair, and the two seatings swap checkpoints, not decks. Over the 200 pairings the first deck's winrate is 0.490 ± 0.018, which is level. The nine pairs share seeds and pairings, so they repeat one draw. The ladder's scores are unaffected.
- **Flat Monte Carlo fails a quarter of its rollouts in four-player** (4,939 of 19,718), mostly "Couldn't map" in `GameCopier`, against 1% in two-player. A third of its four-player decisions fall back to the heuristic. Our search copies through the same class, so the four-player track inherits this.
- **The cost script's first run wrote a Forge profile under `../forge-upstream/forge-gui/data`**: a relative `--out` resolved against the JVM's working directory. One pod failed on it. The path is now made absolute; the stray directory was removed and the pod replayed.
- **Forge's "Game Outcome: Turn N" is not the player-turn count.** It read 22 on a game with 43 turn headers. The script counts headers.
