- **The report crashed and the run read `done`.** A dead worker left a cut line in its decision file. The driver pipes the report through `tee`, which hides its exit status. The report is fixed; the driver's status is routed to Build 2.
- The ladder is not monotone at 40 games (5 rounds reads below 4). The pin was confirmed on the read's 197,117 decisions (8.91).
- The AI deadline fired 44 times in 2,500 plain games at 24 workers.
- Step 4 at a 3 GB cap still lost 11 workers to memory; the fleet sat at 50 GB with 12 GB in swap. 4 GB at 24 workers does not fit this box.

- Precon deck files carry foil marks ("+"), split names and two flavor names; the first table build missed 75 names and was rebuilt. The first extended server crashed on a split name.
