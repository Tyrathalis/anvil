  NOT an engine defect; NOT batch-inference jitter (ruled out: the solo
  replay of concurrently-generated games was byte-exact).

  store lanes partition games disjointly = the generation shape the

  stores sequentially at concurrency 10; fresh server (the old one's
  carry FIFO held poisoned state); watchd re-registered; session
  monitor re-armed; an early-parity check gates the first hour.

  connection/channel instead of (g, seat)** — removes the class for
  any future multi-store replay. Standing rule added (never replay

## Session 3: the phased rerun ALSO failed its early-parity gate — and the real root cause was five milestones old ([ADR-0089](../decisions/ADR-0089-mint-replay-integrity.md))

- The pre-registered stop fired: phase 1's first three completed games
  all diverged (single store — the carry-collision theory predicted
  EXACT). Field-level diff at the split changed everything: **an s-id
  offset, not a behavior flip** — (m, t, oi, p) identical, `s` skipping
  exactly +1 per fork point. Content-aligned comparison then showed
  real behavioral divergence creeping in 0–2 forks later.
- **Mechanism 2 (the deep one): `Obs.mark` shares the decision seq
  counter (M2 D4, by design — the comment says so) and the serve's
  sampling noise is keyed (game_seed, s) (M2 D6).** The mint is the
  first workload to replay a fork-free generation under fork-bearing
  replay: every mark consumed an id, every post-fork decision's s
  shifted, every post-fork decision RE-ROLLED its noise → divergence
  at the first tie-prone choice. Two correct-in-isolation conventions,
  five and three milestones old, colliding in the first workload that
  crossed them.
- The single-fork smoke was structurally blind: early-stop cut the
  stream before any re-rolled decision could act. Run-1's evidence was
  BOTH mechanisms stacked (its 3 pre-first-fork divergences were the
  carry collisions — real, and fixed by phasing; everything else was
  the s-shift).
- **Fix: fork commit `f9eadfa8d4`** — Obs.mark carries the next seq
  without incrementing (nothing reads mark.s; readers use _pos).
  Game-path-identical; `--allow-jar-drift` records the generated-on /
  replayed-on pair in the manifest; **the parity witness is the
  ADR-0025-standard proof, and the fix smoke passed it: g1 STRICT
  EXACT (content AND s) through six consecutive fork turns.**
- ADR-0078 contamination flag recorded (multi-fork sweep games' later
  forks certified re-rolled mainlines — ~15–20% of turns; the ~6×
  FUNDED margin dwarfs it, the asterisk is now on record).
- Lane scripts now truncate scratch obs on start (a killed attempt's
  stale frame sat beside the real one in the fix smoke's parity read).
- Mint relaunched from zero on the fixed jar (~27 h phased); watchd
  re-registered (expect one spurious sched-mint-serve GONE at the
  phase boundary — the driver restarts the server); early-parity gate
  re-armed. Standing rule born: every input to the serve path must be
  replay-stable, and the obs seq IS a serve input.
