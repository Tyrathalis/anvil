## Done

- **D4 recipe PINNED** (12 items, [m9-plan](../design/m9-plan.md) "D4 run
  recipe"). The gate itself was already pinned at the 08-21 gate session and
  is untouched; these are run-shape decisions. Headlines: 8x480 with no drill
  campaign and no arms, failure telemetry added but priced by nothing, drill
  scoring folded into the loop, fresh seed base 20261101.
- **Pin 1 — the graft, which was a launch blocker nobody had noticed.**
  `iter-019` carries no `pay_` params; the serve path gates the payment tag on
  their presence (`has_pay`) and iteration 0 serves `--ckpt` DIRECTLY. An
  ungrafted launch would have bridged ZERO payment windows at iter-0: no live
  baseline, no `pay_class` examples in the first ingest, the head appearing
  only from iteration 1 with nobody the wiser. `scripts/graft_pay_init.py`
  builds `data/training/d4-init/last.pt`; verified by REPRODUCTION — scoring
  the grafted ckpt returns the banked post-boundary day-zero numbers exactly
  (positive 2/64, auto-correct 196/214).
- **Pin 2 — the lr, decided by arithmetic rather than preference.** The loop
  takes one optimizer step per `--traj-per-step` (4) trajectories. Run17 ran
  ~1,668 traj/iter (~417 steps); d6-run18, campaign-free, ran **676 traj/iter
  = 169 steps**. Adam displaces ~lr/step under coherent sign, so at the trunk
  1e-5 the fresh head would move **<=0.014 across the entire probe** —
  `pay_bias` pinned at +2.0, `pay_kind_emb` never leaving zero. The probe
  would have measured the step budget and reported it as a formulation
  verdict. `--pay-lr` splits a named param group (rl.py + selfplay
  passthrough); recipe value 1e-3. NO sweep: three arms against one
  pre-registered threshold is the ADR-0058 counted-once trap wearing a new
  hat. Bought instead with head-movement instrumentation.
- **Pin 6 — the payment head's failure channel reaches the loop.** The serve
  path already reason-coded directed execution (ok/salvage/fail) and recorded
  leftover float; none of it was aggregated. Census tallies now carry
  per-DEVIATION fail/salvage/residue (auto picks never execute a directed
  plan, so window denominators would dilute exactly the signal that matters)
  plus `pay_head` displacement from known inits. Priced by NOTHING —
  deterrence pricing is closed at ADR-0062 and a priced failure would
  confound the probe.
- **Pin 10 — the collapse baseline re-derived IN-ERA, for free, and it
  TRANSFERS.** The D1 baseline was pre-boundary; D4's trajectory is
  post-boundary. `m9-rebaselinearm-s0/s1` were already on disk with
  `obs_schema: 2` + census + argmax play over 1,999 games — exactly
  `veto_knowability.py`'s inputs, no new games. Result: knowable 0.5064
  [0.4937, 0.5190] (gate PASS), validity 0.9934, **knowable-veto rate 0.0432
  [0.0417, 0.0447] vs the pre-boundary 0.0435** — and the same formula
  recomputes the OLD store at 0.0435 exactly, so the eras are comparable by
  construction rather than by assumption. The boundary did not move the veto
  channel. [ADR-0063](../decisions/ADR-0063-m9-d1-veto-knowability.md)
  addendum 2.
- **`d6-run18` RAN AND COMPLETED** (8/8 accepted, no halts, ~20 min/iter).
  Full result table in the plan's D4 RESULT block. The short version:
  **FUND (>=7/64) never approached** (max 4/64 at iterations 0-1, decaying
  after); **CLEAN NEGATIVE not satisfied either** — its positive half holds
  everywhere but argmax deviation bottomed at 0.0288 and settled at 0.0396
  against a <2% requirement. The head did not collapse to always-auto.
- **The substantive finding:** the head deviated MORE than day-zero for two
  iterations (0.0863 -> 0.1295) at its 4/64 peak, then retreated monotonically
  while auto-correct climbed to 0.967 (baseline 0.916). **Straight RL taught
  the marginal statistic — "auto is usually right" — not the conditional
  discrimination "here is where auto is wrong."** Corroborated by a banked
  measurement rather than inferred: the certify-time margin distribution put
  over-threshold mass mostly NEGATIVE. Indicated layer = signal density /
  credit, not representation — M6's layer (ADR-0049) and ADR-0062's account
  of vetoes.
- **The head MOVED** (`pay_kind_emb` rms 0 -> 0.0337 monotone; `pay_bias`
  oscillating around +2.0). Pin 2's instrumentation did precisely the job it
  was added for: this is "moved and did not help", not "never moved" — so the
  negative routes to formulation/credit, not to dose.
- **battery `holding_read` fixed + backfilled.** See below.

## Broke / surprised me

- **The battery died at every iteration and I let it.** `holding_read`
  assumed every entry in a window's `opts` is an option dict; payment-window
  options are JSON label STRINGS on the wire. d6-run18 is the first TRAINING
  run to put payment windows into a store the battery walks, so it crashed
  eight times, non-fatally. **Deliberately not fixed mid-run** — the
  no-tree-edits rule exists because the forkserver re-imports, the read is
  diagnostic, and every gate readout lives in monitor.jsonl. Fixed after the
  loop (isinstance guard) and backfilled from the run's own stores, which is
  also the live validation this instrument class gets by local convention.
  **The recovery paid for itself:** run_end then reported a second anomaly
  the run would otherwise have lost — hold-then-cast MOVED 0.237 -> 0.266
  (>3se), TOWARD the heuristic's 0.345 and opposite to run16's 0.233 ->
  0.182.
- **My run17-derived step-budget estimate was 2.5x too high.** 417 steps/iter
  became 169 once the campaign came out — the campaign was contributing most
  of run17's trajectories. It strengthened the pin-2 conclusion rather than
  weakening it, but the estimate should have been derived from the recipe I
  was actually launching, not the one I copied from.
- **A tripwire scare that the history killed.** `tripwire=2` at iteration 2
  fit a tempting story (fresh pay params -> serve/train skew, appearing only
  as the params grew: 0, 0, then 2). The base rate says otherwise — run12 hit
  3 in a single iteration, run13 and run17 scattered 1s. Magnitudes here
  (0.25-0.31 on 1/128 decisions) sit above the 0.2 tolerance but nowhere near
  the O(1) that indicates real skew, and the trajectories are dropped anyway.
  Checking the history before escalating cost one command.
- Mid-run I over-read a 3-point series as an argmax/sampled divergence
  (ADR-0063's shape). It reversed the next iteration; both channels were
  heading to auto together. Small-N reads inside a live run are for
  orientation, not conclusions — the pre-registered read is the read.
