## The findings (D3 experiment 1 resolves POSITIVE)

1. **The o0 re-measure validates the map:** the lost bin is real —
   4.8% winrate, 316/327 still lost under identical re-measurement.
   Selection noise was not the story.
2. **The map's post-error diagnosis is confirmed and quantified:**
   winrate is monotone in fork earliness, 0.048 → 0.356. Decelerating:
   −2 buys +20pp, −4 another +8pp, peak another ~+2pp.
3. **Per-game (323 games labeled in all four arms):** 277 (86%) clear
   0.2 at some earlier fork; 180 (56%) clear 0.45; only 18 lose all 8
   completions everywhere. **263 (81%) have at least one fork point in
   the trainable band [0.25, 0.75]** — the lost-at-crash bin is not
   dead weight, it's mislocated fork turns.
4. **Error localization:** for 75 games the decisive error sits within
   the last 2 turns before the crash; 53 more between −4 and −2; 52
   recover only at peak; 143 never clear 0.45.
5. **No single global offset wins:** o4 and peak recover substantially
   different games (>0.2: o4-only 65, peak-only 56, both 141) ⇒
   **drill fork turns must be selected per game**, and the sweep labels
   themselves are the selector (e.g. latest fork whose rollout winrate
   lands in the trainable band).
6. **The stubborn 46 (≤0.2 in every arm):** smaller crash−peak gap
   (3.7 vs 5.9) and lower peak_v (0.645 vs 0.721) — the profile of
   games lost before the critic ever liked them; consistent with the
   map's luck-locked estimate. These leave the drill pool.
7. Bonus: the sweep adds ~1,300 K=8 ground-truth labels at new fork
   turns — the critic calibration set (critic 0.58 vs truth 0.24 at
   crash windows) just grew 3×.
