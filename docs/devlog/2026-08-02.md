## What broke / was learned

- **The published jars carry a ~39 MB `target/classes` duplication artifact**
  (dirty-tree assembly quirk; my clean-worktree build is 64 MB vs 105 MB with
  identical real content + identical 138 mac-native entries). v14 ships the
  lean jar; nothing at runtime reads jar-internal `target/classes`.
- Wrong-theory graveyard worth keeping: the `LobbyUpdateEvent` shared-mutable
  `slot` race is NOT live (`RemoteClient.send` encodes on the calling thread);
  the storm-explains-teams theory died on the user's timeline correction —
  the echo is self-sustaining without any flood.
- `setText` is used internally for Escape-cancel, so mid-edit guards belong at
  the network-apply call sites, not inside `FTextField.setText`.
