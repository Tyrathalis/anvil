## Broke / surprised me

- **The unread signal is the lesson of the session.** Signal 2 was pinned
  in the plan, survived the gate session, the recipe session and the run,
  and then three documents wrote up D4 without it. It was not hard to read
  — four minutes on stores already on disk. It went missing because the
  headline gate did not depend on it, so nothing in the pipeline was
  looking for it. New standing rule: read every pre-registered signal,
  including the ones the headline gate does not depend on.
- **The confound was manufactured by a recipe pin, one session after the
  gate was pinned.** Dropping the drill campaign was decided on training
  budget and never checked against the readouts it disarmed. The gate
  session and the recipe session being different sessions is exactly how
  it got through. Second standing rule.
- **I nearly published the veto decline as the M9 headline.** kvr −33% with
  separated CIs, the steepest slope on record, in the first run of the
  lineage where vetoes went down instead of running away into a guard halt
  — it fit the theory too well. The control family was one `loop_config`
  grep away and reversed it. Checking the ledger before believing a
  first-of-its-kind result cost one command; this is the run12/run17 arms
  lesson (ADR-0058) arriving through a different door.
- **run18's 8 clean iterations prove less than they look like.** run16 and
  run17 were also quiet through iteration 9 and halted at 16 and 11. Eight
  iterations is inside the window where the runaway has never yet appeared,
  so "no guard halt" is not evidence of stability.
