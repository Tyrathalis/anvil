## Broke / surprised me

- The d4 value join check came back mean |Δ| 0.002 / max 0.013 — larger
  than 4dp rounding. Cause is bf16 autocast batch-composition drift
  (this dump batches 256 across games; the early-doom traces batched
  per game). No outliers at misjoin scale (a wrong position would show
  ~0.5); accepted as numeric noise, noted in the ADR.
- The 2,000-label learning-curve point pre-registered in the plan is
  unreachable inside one era: ~1.87K labels/era minus the 20% game
  holdout leaves ~1.5K. The curve guard still functions (three points,
  clearly rising), but the tranche sizing in the ADR is what actually
  buys the missing right side of the curve.
