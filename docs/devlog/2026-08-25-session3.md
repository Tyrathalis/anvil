## Broke / surprised me

- Direct uniform ceiling measurement is unpowerable at gate scale for
  high-frequency/small-effect windows: the ×7.1 rate multiplier
  amplifies per-turn noise so ±2pp/game needs ~1M rollouts. The
  two-stage ADR-0075 shape is the general answer for frequent-window
  competencies — worth remembering for any future per-turn ceiling.
- The census's mean realized casts (1.75/turn) vs the affordable-set
  sizes is consistent with substantial unused-resource slack — but
  restraint is sometimes correct; the ceiling measurement adjudicates.
