## Broke / surprised me

- **Census `s` is cumulative per worker; obs `s` resets per game.** The
  join looked perfect on game 0 of every worker (846/846) and silently
  missed everything else — the resolved-games-at-multiples-of-15
  pattern gave it away. Join = `s − census_base[g]`.
- `zstandard` stream_reader defaults to stopping at the first frame
  (`read_across_frames=False`) — the store's one-frame-per-game layout
  makes that look like a tiny valid dataset instead of an error.
- **Per-permanent production, not per-ability:** Elegant Parlor
  (Types: Land Mountain Plains) contributed TWO sources via
  subtype-derived abilities — a whole class of duals double-counted.
  Same structure hides Ancient Tomb's Amount$ 2. Fixed as
  choose-one-union + amount units; validity bar caught the residuals.
- Reminder text repeats costs ("Reconfigure {2} ({2}: ...)") — brace
  parsing must stop at the first '(' or '.' or it doubles the cost.
- The engine's `unpayable` is `ComputerUtilCost.canPayCost` after
  legality passes — it encodes the AI's *willingness* (life payments,
  X sizing), not just rules-payability. Exactly the blind-spot families
  ADR-0062 predicted, now measured in baseline data.
- cwd drift bit twice (zsh `==` glob, a `cd data/runs` that leaked into
  a relaunch and killed a background job instantly on a relative venv
  path — caught by the empty-output check).
