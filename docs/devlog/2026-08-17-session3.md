## M9-seed: the veto-as-interface-artifact framing (user insight, 2026-08-18)

Recorded during the run17 gate sweep, for the closeout ADR to route:
**under auto-payment, probing-via-veto is what OPTIMAL play looks like**
— the model has no payment control and no phase-level plan, so the
engine's veto channel is its only affordability oracle, and the re-ask
ladder makes probes free (the user's human analogy: with auto-mana you
tap-and-see; managing sources yourself, you resolve the phase BEFORE
acting). This explains in one stroke: veto elevation strength-neutral
(3 confirmations), the Sec6c pricing pincer (M6 suppression at
0.02/event vs M8 runaway at 0.01/window — no working lambda exists
because the penalty deters optimal behavior), and drill curricula
amplifying the climb (more desire to act = more probes).
Consequences pinned: (1) deterrence family CLOSED (auto-scaling
lambda, harsher pricing — wrong axis); (2) veto rate becomes a
CAPABILITY DIAGNOSTIC — prediction: a payment-aware, plan-conditioned
model shows veto collapse as a side effect, no penalty needed; if M9
builds the mana head and vetoes don't fall, the interface theory is
falsified; (3) sequencing: Sec3c payment head first (cheaper, pure
public info, forces the internal affordability representation), Sec3a
planning second. Dependency carried from ADR-0042: tier-3 search
scores leaves with the critic — audit measured 0.42 ordering on
fresh-era positions vs the 0.94 ceiling; fine for curation, a real
constraint for search-leaf evaluation. Candidate M9 question: does
mana/plan competence move strength, with veto collapse as the
mechanism check. Related instrument (chartered in the veto
discussion): the veto knowability decomposition (knowable-from-public
vs hidden-info-plausible) — under this framing it doubles as the
baseline the mana head's veto collapse gets measured against.
