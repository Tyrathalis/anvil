# 2026-07-31 session 2: Chronicle design round — daily-gacha structure pinned

Pure design session on the parked Chronicle concept (no code). The
collection-mode sketch grew five new dated sections and closed most of the
open structural questions; every decision is user-pinned in
[collection-mode-sketch.md](../design/collection-mode-sketch.md).

## Done

- **Daily gacha structure v1 (Pocket lens).** Core insight recorded: the
  history walk is a pre-authored live-ops calendar (early sets are literally
  Pocket-sized; chase treatments get invented on-screen — foils 1999, mythics
  2008; the daily Chronicle issue is a real-history story beat). Pins:
  timeline ticks **one in-game day per played real day** (no banking, no
  binge, events on 7/30-day schedules); run length ≈ 2–3 years of played
  days; acquisition = free daily ration + LGS randomized daily stock roll +
  trading-site random offers + tournament prizes in product; **one opening
  per day gets full value** (kinder than Pocket's 12h/24h split timers).
- **Slow singles market = the load-bearing scarcity lever** (user): packs
  stay exciting even at late-game 20-packs/day scale because singles lag.
  Adopted **circulation-based supply, tuned slower than the player** — the
  world opens product on a logistic per set (asymptote < 1; `printed −
  opened` IS the sealed market), the player is a genuine supplier on recent
  sets. Dupe protection not needed (market liquidity); pity deferred (store
  credit design shelved, on record).
- **Circulation/market model v0.** Endpoint-backout adopted (back intrinsic
  value out of stretched present-day Scryfall prices, run history forward);
  single global popularity curve as master demand driver + per-card override
  multipliers (also the Reserved-List-as-event mechanism; "no Reserved List"
  prestige = drop one event). **Exploit-resistance ranks above realism**:
  buylist keys off smoothed reference prices; cracking packs is
  instant-EV-negative by construction (the anti-exploit invariant and the
  Ante ethics ledger are the same number); standing degeneracy-review gate.
- **Collectible identity pinned:** (name, set, collector number, finish);
  language a dormant schema field; condition/grades never in inventory
  identity (noted: could live on market listings/bulk lots later); oddities
  = scripted trophies, not schema. Digital-only printings excluded.
- **MVP slice spec v0:** content window **1993–94 Alpha→Fallen Empires**
  (~9 releases, all era collation confirmed incl. 8-card boosters;
  Chronicles crash held for when the market exists); thin economy (static
  buylist + LGS + daily stock roll); kitchen-table play = stretch
  (ownership-legality only — predates formats); battler NOT in MVP (staging
  sketch reordered: collector loop is the vertical slice). Day-one moment =
  a starter deck. Dogfood gate: 2 weeks of real daily use — which implies
  **Android packaging for the playable branch** (pipeline is
  desktop-jar-only today).
- **Release-event structure:** events / riders / streams (primary releases
  weekly; precons/promos/un-sets attach as side-releases; Secret Lair maps
  to the trading-sites surface). Emergent deceleration: fixed weekly cadence
  × primary-product density = later years stretch, free. Scope pin:
  **collectibility does not require Forge playability** (binder-only flag).
- **Binder UX v0 + two-agent archaeology.** Upstream GitHub sweep: field
  clear — nobody is building a collection browser, zero reveal work; watch
  #10636 (deck-list browser), #11399/#11400 (RewardScene overlay),
  #11093 (Scryfall-data API); UI guidelines merged May (#10637); **iOS
  #11190 MERGED 07-24**. Codebase dive: everything a binder needs exists
  unassembled (ItemManager set-grouping + collector-order piles,
  CardEdition collector numbers/rarity, grayscale shader for unowned slots,
  favorites, foil rendering, Adventure newCards persistence); four small
  gaps (NEW badge never plumbed to the grid ~10 lines; nothing renders
  unowned cards; group headers lack completion stats; no per-set symbols —
  use booster art). Reveal = RewardActor 3D flip × ConquestRewardDialog
  sequencing (each has half). Design: shelf → spread, continuous scroll
  with grayed-out missing cards (user pin; binder covers/pages demoted to
  cosmetics). Foil shimmer debuts WITH the 1999 event — UX and timeline
  agree for free.
- **Cosmetics scoped, deferred:** by asset cost (Tier A zero-art: booster-art
  covers, framed-card trophies, existing avatar sprites, harvest desktop
  deck-sleeves; Tier B procedural; Tier C original art) and by surface
  (**binder-side before table-side** — playmats/dice wait for play surfaces
  by construction; MTG's coin-analog is dice/counters). 3D ambitions
  (tactile pack rips, 3D play mode, dioramas) recorded as far-stretch.
- **Upstream strategy decided (user): fork-first, re-cut later.** Build
  freely on the fork, cut synthetic reviewable commits when upstreaming —
  the workflow the hardening track and #11203 already proved (advisories
  merged byte-identical; 14-file→2-file re-cut taken whole). Riders:
  isolation convention (own packages, Adventure-style, rebase tax ≈ 0);
  standalone prosocial tracks (Scryfall import w/ #11093 coordination,
  Android deltas, era rulesets) keep their own upstream-first schedule;
  Discord concept float softens from gate to courtesy.

## Broke / surprised me

- Nothing broke (no code). Surprises: the emergent-deceleration property
  (weekly cadence × product density = the deceleration curve designs
  itself); Quest's `getCompletionPercent` is the ONLY per-set completion
  math in the whole codebase and it's never been rendered as a grid; the
  NEW badge has existed as a column for years and never reached the image
  grid.
