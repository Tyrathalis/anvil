## Broke / surprised me

- The final crash tally rose to 516/67,880 (0.76%) but stayed
  perfectly point-shaped to the end (39 points; game 423 contributed

- **Hunt RESULT (same evening): 39/39 poisoned points, 156/156
  completions, ZERO crashes under a fresh JVM draw.** The
  state-locality question is ANSWERED: the poison is VARIANT-RANDOM,
  not position-intrinsic — the states that crashed 56/56 in the lanes
  are healthy under a different identity-hash arrangement. Refined
  account: an ORDERING-SENSITIVE copy+resume defect (hash-keyed
  iteration during GameCopier copying occasionally yielding an
  inconsistent copy), trigger = the per-JVM identity-hash lottery ×
  ~2% of points per draw. Naming the concrete exception needs a
  caught instance; the dedicated chase is at its cost wall (every
  attempt = a run-scale lottery draw). **Patient path adopted:
  `crash_why` is standard on every future crash row — the next big
  run names the class for free.** A FIXED_HASH (`-XX:hashCode=3`)
  full-plan rerun is the named escalation if it ever needs forcing
  (deterministic variant ⇒ reproducible poisoned points), priced at
  a full probe-scale run. Server torn down, watchd unregistered.
