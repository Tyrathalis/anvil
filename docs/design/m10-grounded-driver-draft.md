Session: the post-probe2 build round, deepened. This document executes

re-frames the round: the routed items ("calibration fix, then mint

## A. The requirement, read from the probe pair

Probe1 and probe2 are two arms of one experiment. Each driver failed on
exactly the properties the other had:

| conditioning driver | grounded | dense/fresh | on-dist | outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| probe1: decode on own emissions | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ | degenerate fixed point at empty (ADR-0085) — but content_flip **0.0138** = 2.8× the kill bar in one iteration |
| probe2: 170 fixed seed windows | ✓ | ✗ (fitted in ~10 steps) | ✗ (boundary-era) | KL impulse halt; content_flip **0.0019** dead; live decode CE 2.609 → **6.404** |

**The requirement: the driver must be grounded AND dense AND
on-distribution simultaneously.** The 6.404 is the deepest tell — not
"too few labels" but the head pulled actively *off* the live
distribution by an off-distribution fixed batch.

Standing reading pinned here so it can't drift later: probe2's
content_flip 0.0019 below the KILL bar is **not a channel-KILL** — the
channel has never been tested under a sound driver. Probe1's 0.0138
remains the existence proof that content consumption is reachable at
D6 speed. The ADR-0084 gates read on probe3 unchanged.

The shape that satisfies all three properties is DAgger-shaped:
**engine-certified schedule labels minted on the learner's own
trajectory windows, refreshed as the policy moves**, feeding the decode
head densely at emission rows. This sits squarely on the constitution
("the engine adjudicates every claim any learned component makes";
provenance-traced to real games) and satisfies the ADR-0085 standing
rule by construction — the grounded anchor *is* the dense term, and
because targets come from the certifier rather than own emissions, the
empty fixed point is not self-reinforcing.

## B. Fork 1 — the calibration fix (loop-wide; lands regardless of M10)

The defect, precisely (rl.py seedlab path, the ADR-0057 discipline):
raw is measured pre-application (`grad=False`) over the calib window,
`w = frac × mean_pg / raw` is set against raw = 2.73 and **frozen**;
the first ~10 applied steps then deliver full frac-scale gradient on a
maximally-learnable objective, the batch fits (CE → 0.046), and
`seedlab_share` decays *with* the fit — telemetry starts measuring
after the mass is spent.

**Proposed mechanics (conjunctive, all three cheap):**

1. **Per-step subsampling**: the term applies each optimizer step on a
   k-window sample without replacement (reshuffle per epoch), never
   the full batch. Sizing rule below (C) makes memorization slower
   than the iteration by construction.
2. **Warmup ramp on w**: linear 0 → w over the first ~200 applied
   steps. Spreads whatever impulse survives subsampling.
3. **A guard that can SEE the impulse** (the current share guard is
   structurally blind, ADR-0087): a **memorization tripline** — raw CE
   on the term's batch falling below 0.5× its calibration value within
   the first 100 applied steps ⇒ halt (a fixed batch fitting that fast
   IS the defect signature); plus the first post-application telemetry
   row logged at fine granularity so the early share is on the record.

**Considered and REJECTED as the primary fix: per-window w
recalibration.** As raw → 0 on a memorizable batch, recalibration
grows w toward the 1e-4 clamp (potentially ~10³×) to hold the share at
frac — it fights memorization by *amplifying* the fitted term.
Freeze-at-calibration stays; the fix is batch structure + ramp +
tripline, not the freeze.

**Paylab, audited in the same pass (lean: uniform application).** Same
structural shape — a fixed label batch applied per step. Its curve
(3.85 → 0.77 over probe iterations) is slower but the same genre. One
code path, one guard class, applied to every fixed-batch term
(seedlab-successor, paylab); the audit step is to pull paylab's
step-level CE curve from the banked probe1/probe2 artifacts and
confirm the signature's speed before pinning its k.

## C. Fork 2 — the driver rebuild (the deeper solve)

The seedlab-as-primary design is replaced by **trajectory-scale
certified decode supervision**:

- **Substrate**: the `m10-probe1` + `m10-probe2` iteration stores
  (~200 MB total, generated by the init/near-init ckpts — for probe3
  iteration 0 the policy IS the init ckpt, so these are
  on-distribution *by construction*, not by argument). The paygoals4
  census store is admissible secondary material (same serve era).
- **Machinery — all existing**: `schedule_sweep.py` sample→certify
  (h2 composite, THETA 2.0, CONSISTENT 0.75, K_ROLLS — the ADR-0075
  pins verbatim), `seed_sched_labels.py` mint with the select/score
  winner's-curse split verbatim, `seedlabels.build_seed_batch` exact
  rejoin. The new work is pointing the sample at the probe stores and
  running at scale — no new certification logic.
- **Scale — sized by a rule, not a number**: mint M windows such that
  at the chosen k, **no window is visited more than ~2× per
  iteration**. Draft target **M ≈ 2,000–5,000** (the ADR-0075 universe
  was 5,076 — proven scale for this machinery). At M = 2,000 / k = 16
  that is 125 steps per epoch; the 170-batch fitted in ~10 full-batch
  steps, so the epochs-per-iteration margin is the whole point.
- **Mint-cost bench, pinned PRE-build**: time the certifier per window
  on a 50-window sample from a probe store. Decision rule: a
  2,000-window mint ≤ one overnight `nice -19` job ⇒ **per-era
  re-mint** is the cadence; ≤ ~1 h ⇒ the async one-iteration-lag
  labeler is on the table for M10 promotion scale. No cadence is
  adopted before this number exists.
- **Hold coverage rides the distribution**: certification over sampled
  live windows carries the natural pure-hold rate (~6.5% certified
  base) instead of 11 fixed rows — hold-vs-emit supervision scales
  with the mint for free.

## D. Fork 3 — mass sizing

The retired dense term's effective decode mass: `--sched-frac` 0.1 ×
decode's share of the day-zero bundle (2.609/4.93 ≈ 0.53) ≈ **frac
0.053** — the mass that drove content_flip to 0.0138. ADR-0086's 0.1
doubled it into the impulse. **Lean: the new grounded term at frac
0.05**; E/R stays at `--sched-frac` 0.05 (mass-preserving, unchanged
from R7). The ADR-0085 comparable-mass rule is satisfied identically —
the grounded term is the dense term.

## E. Fork 4 — staleness telemetry + the refresh trigger

Pre-registered instrument: **live-gap ratio** = decode CE on live
trajectory emission rows ÷ decode CE on the mint batch, per accepted
iteration. Day-zero reference ≈ 1.0 (2.609 live vs 2.73 batch);
probe2's terminal signature was **139×** (6.404 / 0.046). The live
side is free: R7 kept the loader minting the trajectory decode targets
(counters live, no loss reads them) — the retired term's target
pipeline becomes the staleness instrument, read `grad=False`.

**Draft trigger: gap > 3× sustained over two accepted iterations ⇒ the
batch has staled.** Within the probe, firing routes the online
per-iteration labeler by name (it is NOT built this round); at
promotion scale, the trigger is the re-mint clock. A read, never a
guard — it cannot halt.
