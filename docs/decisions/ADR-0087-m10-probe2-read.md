# ADR-0087: m10-probe2 read — the fixed-batch calibration impulse; certified seed labels alone do not carry the conditioning channel

- **Date:** 2026-08-29
- **Status:** accepted
- **Design-doc anchor:** m10-build-spec §4/R7; reads the [ADR-0086](ADR-0086-emission-grounding.md) surgery, gates from [ADR-0084](ADR-0084-m10-probe-preflight.md)

## Context

`m10-probe2` (the ADR-0086 emission-grounding relaunch; fresh name/seed
20280829/port 50075, recipe otherwise probe1-verbatim) **GUARD-HALTED at
iteration 0** on `kl_mu 0.06128 > 0.06`. Checkpoint rejected,
`loop_state` unchanged, deterministic halt. Read rather than amended —
the ADR-0085 precedent (stop and read; never raise the bar the probe
exists to enforce).

## The forensics (all numbers from banked artifacts)

**The halt is not a marginal overshoot — KL rises within the
iteration.** Step rows, probe2 iteration 0: 0.027 → 0.066 → 0.050 →
0.043 → 0.072 → 0.046 → 0.050 → 0.094 → **0.103** (ending at 1.7× the
bar; mean 0.061). Probe1's iteration 0 rose too, but from and to half
those values (0.035 → 0.051, mean 0.034). Probe2's KL runs ~2× probe1's
throughout.

**Mechanism: the fixed 170-window seed batch is MEMORIZED in ~10
optimizer steps, and the calibration discipline front-loads its mass.**

| seedlab CE | probe1 (frac 0.05) | probe2 (frac 0.1) |
|---|---|---|
| at calibration (pre-application) | 2.730 | 2.728 |
| first measured after applying | 0.512 | 0.421 |
| end of iteration 0 | 0.290 | **0.046** |

ADR-0057 calibration measures the raw term *before* it is applied
(`grad=False` during the calibration window), sets `w` against that
2.73, then freezes it. The first handful of applied steps therefore
deliver full frac-scale mass; the term then collapses as the small fixed
batch is fitted. At double weight the impulse doubles, and it lands on
the **shared trunk**. Decisively: `seedlab_share` *decays* as
memorization proceeds (0.070 → 0.017), so **the share guard is
structurally blind to this** — the mass is spent before the telemetry
window measures it. The guard never could have fired.

**The conditioning channel went the WRONG way.**

| | probe1 iter-0 | probe2 iter-0 | bar |
|---|---|---|---|
| content_flip | 0.013786 | **0.001909** | KILL <0.005 / FUND ≥0.02 |
| argmax_flip (presence) | 0.030541 | 0.044751 | floor 0.012513 |
| decode CE on trajectory emission rows | 1.140 | **6.404** | day-zero 2.609 |

Presence-heavy movement with the content channel quiet: the trunk moved,
but the policy is **not** reading schedule content more — the exact
pattern the ADR-0084 standing rule names as unfundable. Meanwhile decode
CE on real emission rows degraded well past day zero: the head fits 170
certified boundary-era arms and generalizes poorly to live rows.

**Read together: the retired own-emission term was doing real
conditioning work** despite its degeneracy — probe1's iteration-0
content movement at 2.8× the kill bar was that term — and 170
memorizable boundary-era windows do not replace it as a
content-consumption driver.

**What we did NOT learn:** whether the surgery fixes the emission
collapse. Iteration 0's emissions are generated by the *init* ckpt, so
the serve counters (pure-hold 6.01%, full-6 71.4%, mean length 4.85)
merely reproduce day zero (ADR-0085 banked 6.3% / 72%). The
post-surgery emission test lives at iteration 1's generation, which
never ran. **The emission-collapse question remains OPEN.**

**Healthy/unchanged lines:** tripwire 0; E 0.361 and R 0.371 both under
their FUND bars; paylab positive CE 0.766 (probe1 iter-0 0.77); holdout
auto 96.7% flat; 8 BridgePoisonedException skips = the known watch-list
family at unchanged rate; paymark follow 2.92% (probe1 iter-0 3.43% —
within noise at one iteration, and the mark is untrained under the
mask).

## Decision (user-adjudicated 2026-08-29)

1. **The probe closes HALTED-AND-READ at iteration 0. No relaunch on an
   amended KL guard** — the halt is a real drift signal, not a
   threshold artifact.
2. **ADR-0086's "170 is enough" lean is FALSIFIED as a probe premise.**
   The per-era re-mint routed there as a follow-on is **promoted to a
   prerequisite** for any relaunch that leans on seed labels as the
   primary decode signal.
3. **A defect is named, independent of M10:** ADR-0057 auto-calibration
   is unsound for a *fixed small batch applied every step* — calibrate-
   then-freeze on a memorizable term delivers an unmeasured impulse,
   and the share guard cannot see it. → standing rule; the fix (per-
   window recalibration vs freeze-at-calibration, and/or per-step
   subsampling so a small batch cannot be fitted in ~10 steps) is the
   next round's first build item, ahead of any M10 relaunch.
4. **ADR-0086 stands as the as-built record of the surgery** (the
   retirement, the promotion, the restated gate) and is amended by this
   ADR on the frac-0.1 pin and the mint-scale lean; it is not reverted.
   The open question it leaves — does grounded emission hold
   non-degenerate — is untouched by this halt.

## Consequences

- M10 returns to a short build round: the calibration fix first, then
  the mint-scale decision, then relaunch (`m10-probe3`, fresh name per
  the ADR-0076 rule). The ADR-0084 gates and the ADR-0086 restated
  decode leg carry unchanged; no graft change ⇒ floors still stand.
- **Open design tension recorded honestly:** the own-emission term is
  degenerate (ADR-0085) *and* was the content-consumption driver
  (this ADR). A grounded dense term — decode supervised on
  certified/minted schedules at *trajectory scale*, not a 170-window
  fixed batch — is the shape both findings point at. That is the
  re-mint's real justification, not just label freshness.
- Assets banked: iteration-0 full telemetry (rejected ckpt kept),
  the seedlab memorization curve, the probe1/probe2 iteration-0
  paired comparison (same seed shape, one variable pair changed).
- The probe-first discipline pays again: two halted iterations total
  across both probes bought a named calibration defect and a falsified
  premise, at ~25 minutes of compute.
